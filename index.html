My Journey

The Beginning

I started programming in 2019 by building a website for Minhaajushshoobiriin Islamic School, followed by a simple attendance system in PHP. Those first projects taught me the basics of how data is captured, stored, and reported.

At the same time, I pursued a degree in Information Systems, which gave me the foundation in database design, systems analysis, and software development that I still use today.

The Evolution

Over time, I moved on to larger systems. JAPATI (Jaringan Aplikasi Anti Putus Sekolah) was built for the Bogor City Education Office to track and re-enroll students who had dropped out of school, with city-wide tracking and geolocation features.

I later developed SILAK, a financial reporting system for the Bogor City Public Works Office (PUPR), and an E-Report and Attendance System for PT Narwastu Asia Ekatama. Across all of them, my focus was the same: collect data reliably and turn it into reports people can act on.

The Now

Today, I work as a Full Stack Engineer and Data Analyst. I build the applications that capture data, design the databases behind them, and analyze that data to support better decisions.

I am continuing to deepen my data skills with SQL, Python, and BI tools, and exploring modern data engineering tools such as Airflow, BigQuery, and dbt.

My mission is to build systems that make a real impact on people's lives, just like JAPATI did for students in Bogor.

I'm seeking a role where I can build reliable web systems and turn data into insight, while continuing to grow.

With 5+ years of full-stack experience and a strong interest in data analytics, I'm ready to bring both sides to new challenges and opportunities.

Highlighted Projects & Results (Portfolio Pieces)

SILAK – Financial Reporting Information System (Dinas PUPR Kota Bogor)

Problem: Financial reporting and monitoring at the Bogor City Public Works and Spatial Planning Office needed to be more accurate, transparent, and timely.

What I built: A web-based system for financial data entry, validation, monitoring, and report generation, providing real-time financial data to support decision-making and accountability. I worked as a programmer expert, responsible for development and implementation in line with government standards.

JAPATI – Anti Dropout Student Tracking

Problem: The Bogor City Education Office needed a way to identify out-of-school children and bring them back into education.

What I built: A web-based application for registering and tracking students with geolocation, supporting placement in nearby schools and PKBM. I contributed as a programmer expert, developing and implementing the system.

Result: By November 2019, more than 100 students had been registered and placed, with all costs funded by the government.

E-Report & Attendance System – PT Narwastu Asia Ekatama (NAE)

Problem: NAE, a pest control company, needed field technicians' reports and attendance to be tracked in real time for both the admin team and management.

What I built: A web-based E-Report and Attendance System with structured reporting, automated attendance monitoring, and admin and management views, improving accuracy and transparency in daily operations.
